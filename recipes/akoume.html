Akoumè and adémè

Presentation of the recipe

This dish above is the most emblematic dish in Togo
It's corn paste called "Akoumè" eaten with a soup
made of some vegetables called "adémè" with a bunch
of meet and other condiments used in a specific way
to have a very delicious soup.

Ingredients

  1. 500ml of water
  2. a bowl of maize flour
  3. somme jew's mallow
  4. some red palm oil
  5. salt
  6. meat or braised fish
