Akoumè and adémè

Presentation of the recipe

This dish above is the most emblematic dish in Togo
It's corn paste called "Akoumè" eaten with a soup
made of some vegetables called "adémè" with a bunch
of meet and other condiments used in a specific way
to have a very delicious soup.

Ingredients

  • 500ml of water
  • a bowl of maize flour
  • somme jew's mallow
  • some red palm oil
  • salt
  • meat or braised fish

Steps for the akoume ( corn paste )

  1. mix a little bit of the flour with some water till you obtain a smooth and uniform paste
  2. put the water on the fire and wait
  3. when the water starts boiling , add the paste you obtained earlier and mix till it starts boiling again
  4. now , you add some flour progressively while mixing it according to how though you want it to be at final.

Steps for ademe ( jew's mallow soup)

  1. arrange the leaves in order to take away the stems and the waste
  2. put the water on the fire and wait till it starts boiling
  3. put the leaves in the boiling water and hit it in order to tear the leaves
  4. add the salt and the oil and the other condiments to have the soup ready
