White Rice

Description

Rice is just a simple dish made by putting raw rice in boiling water
till it becomes smooth and eatable

Ingredients

  • A bowl of rice
  • Salt
  • Some water

Steps of cooking

  1. put the water of he fire till it boils
  2. add salt to the boiling water
  3. wash the rice properly and put the rice in the boiling water
  4. add some oil
  5. wait that it gets cooked
